Poképals — macOS app icon concepts

Five directions to replace the generic system icon, drawn from the ivory / warm-timber / garden art direction. Squircle mask approximates the real macOS icon shape.

1 · Garden window

A pet peeking through the timber-framed window over the garden — the room scene compressed into one glance.

2 · Poké Ball badge

Classic ball centered with two ears breaking the silhouette — reads instantly as "Pokémon pet" at any size.

3 · Gold call button

The in-game gold "call" control, enlarged, with a paw print at its core. Ties the icon directly to the core interaction.

4 · Kennel plate

A doghouse with a blank gold nameplate — echoes onboarding/naming and reads well as a small dock icon.

5 · Three-portrait cluster

Pikachu, Mudkip and Charmander as overlapping color swatches over a garden strip — communicates "roster of pets" at a glance.
